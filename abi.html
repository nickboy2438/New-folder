Chromosome Disorders

Chromosomal disorders occur when there is a change in the number or structure of chromosomes.

Disorder Chromosomal Change Karyotype
Down Syndrome Extra chromosome 21 47,XX,+21 / 47,XY,+21
Edwards Syndrome Extra chromosome 18 47,XX,+18 / 47,XY,+18
Patau Syndrome Extra chromosome 13 47,XX,+13 / 47,XY,+13
Turner Syndrome Missing one X chromosome 45,X
Klinefelter Syndrome Extra X chromosome 47,XXY

Genetic Information

Chromosomes are structures made of DNA. DNA contains genes, which carry genetic information. Changes in chromosome number or structure can cause chromosomal disorders.
